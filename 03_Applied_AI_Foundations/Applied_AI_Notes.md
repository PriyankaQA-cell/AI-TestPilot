# Applied AI Notes

## What is Applied AI?

Applied AI focuses on using AI to solve practical day to day business problems rather than understanding only the theory behind AI.

The goal is to improve productivity by combining human expertise with AI capabilities.

---

## AI as a Collaborator

One of the biggest learnings from this course is that AI should be treated as a collaborator (working with and for you) rather than a replacement for humans.

AI can generate ideas, summarize information, analyze data, and automate repetitive work, but human review remains essential and non-negotiable.

---

## AI Workflows

Complex tasks can be divided into smaller steps where AI assists with specific activities.

Instead of asking AI to complete an entire project, better results are achieved by guiding AI through structured workflows.

---

## Human in the Loop

Human validation is critical before accepting AI-generated output.

Professionals should review AI responses for:

- Accuracy
- Completeness
- Business relevance
- Risks
- Compliance

---

## Effective Prompting

The quality of AI responses depends heavily on the clarity of instructions.

Providing context, defining the role, specifying the task, and requesting structured output significantly improves results.

---

## Responsible AI

AI outputs should always be verified.

Users should avoid sharing confidential information and remain aware of AI limitations such as hallucinations.

---

## My Key Learnings

- AI improves productivity.
- AI is most effective when used collaboratively.
- Human judgment remains essential.
- Better prompts produce better results.
- AI workflows outperform one-shot prompting.
