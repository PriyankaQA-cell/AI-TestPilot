# Applied AI Foundations

## Overview

This module documents my learning from the OpenAI Academy – Applied AI Foundations course.

The course focuses on moving beyond understanding AI concepts and learning how to apply AI effectively in real-world professional workflows.

As a QA Engineer, Project Manager, and Business Analyst, my objective is to use AI to improve productivity, reduce repetitive work, enhance documentation quality, and support better decision-making while ensuring human validation remains an essential part of the process.

---

## Learning Objectives

After completing this module, I can:

- Understand how AI supports workplace productivity.
- Design structured AI workflows.
- Break down complex tasks into manageable AI-assisted steps.
- Write better prompts for professional use.
- Apply AI in QA, Project Management, and Business Analysis.
- Identify tasks that should and should not be delegated to AI.
- Review AI-generated outputs critically before implementation.
