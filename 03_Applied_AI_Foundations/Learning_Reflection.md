# Learning Reflection

## Why I Took This Course

After completing AI Foundations, I wanted to learn how AI could be applied in real workplace scenarios rather than understanding only theoretical concepts.

---

## Biggest Learning

The most valuable lesson was understanding that AI should be integrated into workflows instead of being expected to solve an entire problem independently.

---

## What Changed My Thinking

Initially, I believed AI was mainly useful for generating content.

After completing this course, I realized AI can support decision-making, planning, documentation, testing, and project coordination when used correctly.

---

## What I Found Interesting

Breaking complex work into smaller AI-assisted tasks significantly improves the quality of results.

---

## What I Found Challenging

Designing structured workflows and writing detailed prompts require practice.

---

## How This Helps My Career

This course has helped me understand how AI can increase my productivity as a QA Engineer, Project Manager, and Business Analyst.

I now think more about designing workflows instead of asking isolated questions.
