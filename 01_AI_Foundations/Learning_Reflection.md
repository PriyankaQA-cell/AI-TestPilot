# Learning Reflection

## Why I Chose This Course

As a Manual QA Engineer transitioning into AI-enabled software testing, I wanted to build a strong foundation before learning advanced AI tools.

---

## What I Learned

This course helped me understand the differences between AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI.

I also learned the importance of Prompt Engineering and Responsible AI.

---

## Challenges

Initially, understanding LLMs and Tokens was challenging because these were new concepts for me.

After completing the course and practicing with ChatGPT, these concepts became much clearer.

---

## My Biggest Learning

AI should be viewed as an assistant that improves productivity rather than a replacement for human expertise.

---

## Next Goal

Learn Prompt Engineering and begin applying AI to software testing tasks.
